Kestrel Courier

A courier flight over Caelum Valley. Fly the rings, mind the engine, and bring the Kestrel home to Meridian Field.

How to fly

↑↓ pitch · ←→ bank and turn · W/S throttle · Space brake · V change view · R restart the engine · P pause. Drag on the screen to steer; the buttons on the right work by touch.

A sortie lasts 4 minutes and pays 20,000 points a minute while you hold 120 knots near 1,500 ft. Each gold ring is worth 5,000. At 3.5 minutes the runway lights come on 1.2 km out: land on the centreline, within 10 m, sinking at least 300 ft/min, to win.

The engine fails every 80–120 seconds. When the red warning shows, press R within 15 seconds or the engine stays dead and you glide. Watch the fuel — empty means gliding too.

Three views: cockpit, chase, and the tower. The leaderboard on this page keeps your best flights of this visit only.
